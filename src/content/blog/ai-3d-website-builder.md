## How Does an AI 3D Website Builder Work?

An AI 3D website builder leverages advanced algorithms and machine learning to interpret your text prompts and convert them into stunning visuals. Here’s a breakdown of the process:

### 1. Text Input
Users provide descriptive text prompts that outline their vision. This input can range from simple keywords to detailed descriptions of desired aesthetics.

### 2. AI Processing
The AI analyzes the input, utilizing its vast database of design principles and 3D modeling techniques to create a unique website layout. 

### 3. Customization
After the initial design is generated, users can customize elements such as colors, textures, and animations to better align with their brand identity.

### 4. Launch
Once satisfied, users can publish their websites with just a few clicks, making the process seamless and efficient.
